According to *The Jargon File*, *q.v.*, [the word “luser” was coined at MIT “around 1975”](http://www.catb.org/jargon/html/L/luser.html).  If you think that MIT is a middle school, you must be, like, *super*-super-smart.

I’ve known that for decades, of course—hence my hacker usage of the word.  But for giggles, I just checked [*Wikipedia*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luser).  Cool!  The wiki one-ups ESR, with a link to [source code from 1969 that shows the word “LUSER”](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rcornwell/ctss/master/original/com1) in a context similar to what ESR reports about usage at MIT in 1975.  I’m insufficiently leet to know all of these old systems off the top of my head; research is required to track down any potential relation between these two attested usages.
