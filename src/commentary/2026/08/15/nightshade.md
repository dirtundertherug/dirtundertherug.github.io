For the past few years, there has already been a deployed adversarial system from the University of Chicago [for artists to poison their images in a way that’s more or less imperceptible to humans:  Nightshade.](https://nightshade.cs.uchicago.edu/)  Widely used by actual artists.  However, it’s not open-source, it doesn’t run on Linux—and I’ve seen some preprints floating around of research that claims it’s less effective than advertised.  I do not know how effective it really is.
