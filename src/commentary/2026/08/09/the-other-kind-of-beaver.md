To catch the current *Zeitgeist* with an antiporn angle, Buc-ee’s must sue *Hustler* magazine for the mascot of *Hustler’s* Beaver Hunt:  An anthropomorphic cartoon beaver wearing a hat with an anarchy symbol, chomping a cigar, pointing a camera at the reader.

The consumer confusion and trademark dilution are worsened by the *Hustler* Beaver Hunt mascot’s hat; the Buc-ee’s beaver mascot also wears a hat!  So similar thus are these mascots, Buc-ee’s customers risk being confused into wardrobe malfunctions in Buc-ee’s stores.
