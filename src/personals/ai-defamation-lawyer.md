Title: Seeking clueful Net lawyer re “AI” defamation
Subtitle: Want an interesting case with an “AI” angle?
Author: impurify
Published: 2026-09-30
Impure-Byline: 1

<span>At the time of this article’s publication,</span> an anonymous troll is persistently making repeated statements about me in a public venue that are (a)&nbsp;false in fact, (b)&nbsp;damaging to me.  Per the defamation plaintiff’s classic dilemma, I question the wisdom of amplifying these falsehoods by unnecessarily repeating them.  The upshot is that with no more connection to reality than a stereotypical conspiracy theorist, someone is following me around in multiple discussion threads with false, baseless, malicious statements which, in substantial essence, *deny my humanity*.  It is no hyperbole to say that they’re intentionally trying to unperson me.

Alas for me, my facility with the written word is one of but a few compensatory talents with which I am endowed; it is a valuable talent, it’s pretty much almost all I have, and it is accordingly all the more dear to me.  Whereas the “AI” companies have created a product which is defective by design, which creates mass-scale second-order social harms.  One of those harms is what I’ve termed “[social grade inflation](../neology/gloss.html#social-grade-inflation){ .glosslink }”, [the process by which writing skills are socially devalued][social-grade-inflation-first-statement].

This *implicit* cultural development is deeply distressing to me; it has compelled me to disclaim LLM usage, as I’ve done in this website’s footer [ever since it launched][dutr-first-archive].  What I’ve now found out the hard way is that malicious parties can *explicitly* exploit this cultural development for the purpose of libel—under the cover of Internet anonymity.

Like most people who have a remarkable skill, I would in principle be pleased, and even eager to show it off in open court.  Arguably, I may even be overeager—notwithstanding my usual ire at being expected to prove my humanity.  I detest captchas as degrading, and I revolt in a rage whenever a robot orders me to click a box that says, “I’m a human”—*i.e.*, to prove that I am [one unit of breathing meat](../neology/gloss.html#breathing-meat){ .glosslink }.  But the purveyors of so-called “artificial intelligence” have so aggrieved me, I’d be thrilled to show the world that my natural intelligence is superior to any machine’s.  I don’t need their [mass-plagiarism devices][plagiarism]—their *cheating tools*, all users of which categorically must be shamed, shunned, and expelled from human society!

[plagiarism]: ../copyright/plagiarism.html "impurify (2026-09-23). “Copyright versus Plagiarism: Know the difference for a freer culture, a better public discourse—and a more effective fight against ‘AI’.” *Dirt Under The Rug*."

The anonymous troll’s statements are false in fact, are presented without even a scintilla of valid evidence, and, if it matters, are patently made with a malice that drips like vitriol from their every word.  The *easy* part of this case is to prove the elements of libel.  Moreover, I suggest as a mere hint that the case evokes interesting, and perhaps even novel issues that a lawyer would probably like to discuss with me as much as I want to discuss this with them; at this point, methinks I’d be well-guided not to expound my such thoughts prematurely.

<span id="practical-difficulties">The practical difficulties are of a different nature:</span>

0. It’s been inadvertently leaked that [I need assistance I don’t have with voting][leak]; how am I supposed to lawyer up, go to court, and seek the relief to which people are normally entitled when others harm them?

0. As is not infrequently the case, an antiquated legal system places a pseudonymous prospective plaintiff on the horns of a dilemma; self-doxing to seek relief trades a damaging harm for an irreparable harm.  As will be revealed by the merest glance at my website and my otherwise public commentary, I express controversial opinions—including, but not limited to what First Amendment lawyers call “core political speech”.  As I’d be happy to discuss confidentially with an attorney, I use the shield of pseudonymity for the legitimate purpose of expressing my opinions without the risk of personal harassment, and even violence.

To an experienced Net lawyer with the appropriate sensitivity, navigating these issues should be old hat.  I don’t know how to deal with it; <abbr title="I am not a lawyer">IANAL</abbr>, after all.

If you are such a lawyer, please email me.  This is separate from my search for [First Amendment lawyers or scholars][first-amendment-lawyer]—although of course, the same person may fulfill both roles.
