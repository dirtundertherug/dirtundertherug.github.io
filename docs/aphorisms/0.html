Childhood and Adolescence2

I am irritated when people speak of childhood as if it had no history.

Indeed, it bothers me no end that most people treat modern Western ideas about childhood and adolescence as ahistorical, universal, biologically deterministic Truths. Puberty is to adolescence as genitals are to gender.

Just as much, it bothers me that so many people on both sides of this kind of debate tend to be procrustean. It devolves into a false dilemma between “raise the age for X” and “lower the age for X”. Significant variations in rate of development are treated to the procrustean bed; thereupon, discourse is framed only as a question of making the bed longer or shorter. I felt this acutely as a child with a precocious mind and a retarded body. Whereas variation, a diversity of plural childhoods, is perhaps the only biologically deterministic universal fact of childhood.
