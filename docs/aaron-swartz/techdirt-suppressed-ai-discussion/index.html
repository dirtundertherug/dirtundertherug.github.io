Why?

Why am I troubling myself to restore this discussion to the Open Web? Two reasons: (a) In conscience, for the same reasons stated in my first comment linked above; and, (b) this:

“How long is it going to take before moderation system designers realize that the simple act of trying to repress intelligent, substantive discussion they don’t like online is likely to make it so that something that most people would never, ever see (like a few blog comments posted by an obscure person on a three-year-old Techdirt article) is now seen by many more people? Let’s call it the Masnick Effect.” — impurify, closely paraphrasing Mike Masnick (2005) from his coining of the term “Streisand Effect” (emphasis is Masnick’s).

The singular comment that apparently was worthwhile for someone to suppress shall be on the Open Web, and it shall be on the Open Web forever. So shall the whole discussion.

Although I intend one last comment on the Techdirt thread, which I’ll POSSE here, I will not continue the discussion in the dark.
